\sectiontitle{RESEARCH STATEMENT}
My research develops explainable CNN--Transformer hybrid architectures for high-stakes real-world decision support, spanning clinical medical imaging and urban policy forecasting, where model transparency and reliability are as critical as predictive accuracy. In the medical domain, I design lightweight CNN--Transformer encoders with uncertainty quantification (Monte Carlo Dropout) and visual explainability (Grad-CAM, attention maps) to support trustworthy clinical decision-making in gastrointestinal endoscopy and polyp segmentation. In the urban domain, I extend this interpretability-first approach to multimodal spatial AI, combining street-level and satellite imagery with socioeconomic tabular features in a CNN--Transformer framework to produce explainable, SHAP-driven affordability forecasts that inform evidence-based housing policy. Going forward, I aim to unify these two threads by building generalizable, uncertainty-aware explainable AI systems that bridge visual perception and structured data reasoning, deployable across both clinical environments and societal decision-making contexts where the cost of an opaque prediction is unacceptably high.
